# Peer Review — Story 00000036: Add proposed ruleset three

## Summary

This branch adds a complete second rules major — an 8×8 open board, a generated
starting position, five ranks numbered the opposite way from major 2, and rank
reduction on surviving combat — as a fourth playable game, "Demotion", and opens
a major-agnostic presentation seam so both majors can be live at once. The
repository checks are all clean as of the final commit: `npm run typecheck`
passes, `npm run lint` passes, and `npm test` passes with 1239 tests across 56
files.

The load-bearing constraints hold. `git diff --name-status main...HEAD` touches
no file under `src/rules/primary/v2/`, `src/rules/readRecord*`, `src/engine/` or
`src/encoding/`; `grep -rn "primary/v2" src/rules/primary/v3/` and a
case-insensitive grep of `src/rules/primary/v2/` for `v3`/`major 3` are both
zero hits; `src/board/view/` imports only `vitest`, its own siblings and
`src/art/pieceArt.ts`. The six files under `reference/` each have exactly one
commit in the branch and were never modified after being added. I read the v3
engine against `reference/rules.md` and `reference/start-position.md` section by
section — direction-relative encumbrance (§4.2), the diagonal open-path rule
(§4.4), the formation bonus and rank reduction (§4.3), notation's
one-mark-per-square invariant (§4.5), the `S ≥ 22` threshold written in its
derived form, the position ID kept as a string with validation separate from
decoding, and the ending precedence including mutual attrition before
single-side attrition and the limit of 40 — and found no rules defect. Piece art
is keyed by a `PieceArt` discriminated union on `major`, with one sprite table
per major and per-major exhaustive adapters, so a shared drawing genuinely
cannot become a shared key. Rank is read live from the board everywhere I
checked: legality, the formation bonus (recomputed from the pre-combat position,
never cached), the artwork and corner numeral (both derived from
`piece.rank` on every render), the accessible square label, and the selection
and demotion announcements.

The findings below are two defects in the new `ResignControl` and a set of
smaller documentation, duplication and rot risks. I agree with the recorded
limitations: records are out of scope and nothing is lost because moves are
stored in the extended `x`/`=N` form; the "How to play" primer stays major-2
content; the `2-0:CLASH` divergence is untouched; and **Gate I is only partly
met** — the keyboard half is verified, the screen-reader half is structurally
verified but never heard, so story.md's in-scope item 12 is not fully satisfied.
Comment 1 below is a structural gap that I believe would fail a real
screen-reader pass, and is a new finding rather than a restatement of that
limitation.

## Notes on things checked and found sound

These are not findings; they are recorded so a re-review does not have to redo
the work.

- **Version wall, both directions** — verified by file list and by grep, not by
  trusting the Step 17 note.
- **Reference snapshot** — every file under `reference/` has exactly one commit
  in the branch and no subsequent modification. (Its fidelity to companion
  commit `b3c3202` cannot be checked here; there is no network access.)
- **Rules correctness** — encumbrance direction sets, diagonal flank derivation,
  formation-bonus reach and timing, rank-reduction clamping at 1, Flag capture
  excluded from combat, notation's five forms, the derived `S × 15 > 7 × 45`
  threshold, half-turn/reflection square mappings, position-ID validation with
  no numeric conversion anywhere, ending precedence and the inactivity limit of
  40 all match the pinned reference.
- **Piece art** — `PieceArt` is a discriminated union on `major` with two
  separate tables and two exhaustive per-major adapters; no lookup keys off a
  name or a bare digit, and `PieceIcon` imports nothing from `src/rules/`.
- **Major 2 unchanged** — `GameResult`'s `data-` attribute values, `FullBoard`'s
  square labels and lake handling, and the picker's three visible
  name/description strings are all preserved through the refactor; the relocated
  orientation tests are a net gain (33 cases before, 35 after).
- **README step** — the plan does include a step to verify `README.md`
  (Step 18), so there is nothing to raise on that count.
